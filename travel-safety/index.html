    Safety Tips for Travellers

    Practical advice to stay secure on the road.

    Stay Aware

    Keep your phone charged and avoid walking alone in unfamiliar areas at night.

    Share Your Location

    Share live location with a trusted contact before and during long journeys.

    Secure Your Belongings

    Use RFID-blocking wallets and keep valuables close, never in back pockets.

    Verify Transport

    Confirm ride details and share the trip with someone before you board.

    Safe Accommodation

    Choose reputable stays and always check room locks and exits on arrival.

    Carry Essentials

    Keep a whistle, power bank, and a compact first-aid kit within reach.
